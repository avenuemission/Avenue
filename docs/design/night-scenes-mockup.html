One avenue, four views

Each page gets a small night scene in the same style as the leader journey. Vision shows the whole avenue. Pathways, Intersection and Streetlights each zoom into one part of it. Every scene builds once when it scrolls into view, then gently glows.

1 · Vision (v2)A bird's-eye view of a whole suburb at night. One light at the central crossroads. Homes across the suburb light up (pathways), lamps come on along their streets (streetlights), light travels to where neighbours meet and blooms (intersection), then flows back along the streets to the centre, which glows brighter.
The model

One light, many homes

Everything begins with Jesus, the light at the centre of it all.

Pathways are house churches: small communities meeting in homes, growing together around Him. From every home, streetlights reach out into the neighbourhood. And where pathways cross, they meet at the intersection to worship God in one spirit.

PathwaysIntersectionStreetlights
2 · PathwaysOne home on the avenue. People walk in from both ends of the street, and the windows light up as they gather. Then two people are sent out, one each way, and the houses on either side light up: the group has multiplied. People now walk at an unhurried pace.
How it works

Church around a table

A pathway is a house church. A few people meet in a home, a library or a school, wherever God has placed them.

We eat together, read the Bible together, pray and look out for one another. When a pathway grows, it sends people out to light up the next home, so the avenue keeps getting brighter.

3 · IntersectionA crossroads from above. Light travels in from homes on all four streets and meets in the middle, where a warm circle blooms and stays glowing.
Where roads meet

Where pathways meet

Each pathway walks its own street, and every street leads back to Jesus.

When our paths cross, we gather as one family: weekly Prayer and Praise, combined pathway nights, quarterly sermons, Avenue camps and plenty of fun in between. Many homes, one voice.

4 · StreetlightsThe street seen from the side, like the journey screen. From one lit home, the lamps switch on one by one down the street, and each pool of light reveals the people it reaches.
Light for the street

Every pathway lights its street

God has placed each pathway in its neighbourhood on purpose. Streetlights are how it shines there.

Homeless outreach, songwriting, podcasts, mission trips, partnerships with local churches and organisations. Avenue helps every group turn a God-given idea into light for the people around them.
